Our proposal

Gingerbread Christmas Village

We propose to design, supply on rental, install, maintain and dismantle the complete 12 × 12 m Gingerbread Christmas Village for AED 190,000 excl. VAT (AED 199,500 incl. 5% VAT), delivered turnkey in 14 working days from approval plus timing on site.

The offer follows Seadrop's reference concept and item list in full, and adds premium upgrades: a fully carved 3D Santa house, carved icing on all four arches, a cookie-decorating workshop, a second giant teddy and animated snowfall on the main tree.

Every element is free-standing and ballasted, with nothing suspended from the ceiling.

Layout & look

The village plan

The 5 m tree stands at the centre, with an entrance arch on each side so visitors can enter from any direction of the atrium.

The four corners hold the themed zones: Santa house and bakery at the back, teddy photo corner and cookie workshop at the front. Clear 3.5 m paths from each arch to the tree keep Santa and photo queues away from the fence line.

Look and feel: warm gingerbread-brown walls with piped white icing on every edge, snow-topped roofs with candy details, pastel ornaments in pink, mint and gold, and warm-white 3000 K lighting.

12 × 12 m plot · four 4.0 m arches · 3.5 m paths · 8 secondary trees · 32 m fencing

Investment

How the AED 190,000 is used

The full base scope from Seadrop's reference concept stays unchanged. The additional budget goes into the elements visitors see, photograph and spend time in.

We still keep costs efficient where it doesn't show: trees, teddies, furniture and décor come from our rental stock, the Santa house and bakery are built on our reusable steel frames, and the fencing uses printed FR MDF panels.
